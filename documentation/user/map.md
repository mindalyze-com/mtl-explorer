# Use the map

The map shows all tracks that match your current [filter](filters/README.md).

## Find out what's at a spot

Click anywhere on the map. **Choose what to see here** lists everything at that
spot: your GPS tracks, photos, places, and public routes. **Your GPS tracks** are
your own recordings; **Waymarked routes** and **Swiss routes** are public trail
networks.

- Click a category to see all its results. Use **Previous** and **Next** for
  long lists.
- Point at one of your tracks in the list to highlight it on the map.
- Click a result to open its details. **Back to results** returns to the list.

If nothing is there, you'll see **Nothing found here**.

## Open a track

Click a track, or choose it from the list. Its details open in a panel. The
address bar changes to `/track/…`, so you can bookmark or share the track.

On a phone, the details fill the screen. Tap **Restore panel size** before
dragging the panel down.

See [track details](tracks/details.md) for what each tab shows.

## Open photos

Click a photo marker or a group of photos. Under **Photos**, choose the photo
you clicked, **This cluster**, or **Map view at tap** (all photos visible when
you clicked). To see the photos of one activity, use the track's **Media** tab.

Turn photos and videos on in **Map → Your data**. See
[browse photos and videos](media/browsing.md).

## Change what the map shows

Click **Map** to:

- choose a base map,
- turn your own data on or off: tracks, photos, and videos,
- add **Points of interest** such as peaks, viewpoints, drinking water, huts,
  food, and transport,
- turn on 3D **Terrain**,
- add **Route overlays** such as Waymarked Trails.

Public trails and points of interest are for reference only. They are not added
to your tracks or your plans.

Click a public route to see its name, activity, and a link to more information.
Swiss routes also link to their stages. For unnamed Swiss trails, use
**Open in Swiss map**. If routes can't be identified, zoom in.

Use [Filter](filters/README.md) to choose which tracks appear, and
[coloring](filters/coloring.md) to change how they look. A heatmap shows where
you go most often.

## The status indicator

The small indicator on the map shows when data is loading or processing, when
the map is out of date, and when you are offline. Click it for details, and
press Esc to close it.

If it says **N files need review**, click **Review** to check them. **Dismiss**
hides the notice for these files; use **Undo** if you change your mind. New
problems will show up again.

See [Review](tracks/source-review.md) and [offline use](mobile-and-offline.md).
