# Browse photos and videos

There are three ways to find your photos:

- **On the map:** turn on photos in **Map → Your data**, then click a marker.
  Under **Photos**, choose the photo you clicked, **This cluster**, or
  **Map view at tap** (all photos visible when you clicked).
- **By activity:** open a track's **Media** tab.
- **By period:** in **Statistics → Trends**, click a media bar.

## The viewer

If a thumbnail shows **Retry**, select it to load the image again. Once it loads,
select the thumbnail to open the viewer.

- Move between photos with the side buttons, arrow keys, or by swiping.
- Zoom and pan the photo.
- **View** shows or hides **Details**, **Location map**, and **Photo strip**.
  **Photo only** hides them all; **Restore panels** brings them back.
- **More** shows technical information.
- **Expand** makes the viewer bigger; **Full screen** fills the screen. Press
  Escape or **Exit full screen** to go back.
- Download the original file.

**All media** shows every photo in the time span of your filtered tracks;
**Track related** shows only photos linked to a track. Photos without a date
don't appear in date-based views.
