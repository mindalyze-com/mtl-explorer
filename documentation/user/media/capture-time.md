# Correct camera time

If your camera clock was wrong, photos are placed at the wrong spot on a track.
You can correct the time offset.

1. Open the track's **Media → Photo tools**.
2. Enter the camera offset and choose **Preview**.
3. Check the photos on the current page.
4. Choose **Save correction**. It applies to the photos on this preview page
   only, not to every photo from that camera.

**Reset** discards an unsaved preview. To remove a saved correction, open
**Photo tools** and choose **Clear clock correction** next to it.

If a photo has GPS time, that time is used instead. The photo file itself is
never changed. Matching needs a track with real timestamps. It may take a moment
until the photos move.
