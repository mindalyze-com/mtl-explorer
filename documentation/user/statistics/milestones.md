# Milestones and exploration

**Overview** shows milestones such as your first 100 km or 100 miles, depending
on your units.

The **Exploration score** shows how much of an activity was new to you, compared
with your earlier tracks. It is shown as a percentage.

The score only knows your imported tracks, not every place you have been.
Duplicates, hidden tracks, and GPS gaps affect it. Importing older tracks can
change the score of later ones once they are recalculated; check
**Settings → Processing**.

See [the calculation](../../architecture/analysis/exploration.md) for details.
