# Compare trends

Open **Statistics → Trends** to compare your activity over time.

1. Group by total, year, quarter, month, week, or day.
2. Use the period controls to focus on the time you want.
3. Read the charts, or sort the table below them.

Chart settings let you change chart height and order. They are saved in this
browser and can be reset.

## What the values mean

- **Duration** is moving time.
- **Energy** is estimated work in Wh. Power, Normalized Power, Intensity Index,
  and Training Load are based on the same estimate.
- Intensity and load depend on your threshold power. If none is set, 150 W is
  used, so they may not match your fitness.
- Calories from individual tracks are not added up here.

See [energy estimates](../analysis/energy.md).

## Photos and videos

Photo and video counts cover the time span of your filtered tracks. **All media**
counts every photo in that span; **Track related** counts only photos linked to a
track. Click a media bar to see its photos.

Health trends have their own dates in **Health**; changing the track filter
doesn't change them.
