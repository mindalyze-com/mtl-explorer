# Moving time and breaks

A track's **Overview** splits its time into moving, stopped, and unknown.
Unknown means the GPS data can't tell whether you moved or paused.

Short pauses count as stopped time. Pauses of one minute or more also appear as
breaks in **Events**. Their start and end are approximate.

Open **Track details → Events** and click a break or GPS gap to see it on the
map.

A GPS gap doesn't prove you stopped; positions are simply missing. Missing or
wrong timestamps can make the total time unavailable, even if you include the
track in [Review](../tracks/source-review.md).

The normal average heart rate includes stops. The moving average heart rate
only counts time in motion.
