# Read energy estimates

Open a track's **Overview → Energy**. You see the estimated active calories and
the mechanical work. Expand **Energy details** for total calories, how much of
the track is covered, and **Adjust body weight**.

- **Active calories** are what you burned on top of resting.
- **Total calories** include resting.
- **Mechanical work** (in Wh) is the physical work of moving.

## Change the body weight

Enter a weight for this track, check the preview, and click **Save**. **Reset**
goes back to the saved value. This only changes this track, not your default.

## Power

**Energy details** also shows estimated power: average moving power, Normalized
Power, and Max 30 s Power. They are calculated from GPS, not measured, so they
can differ from a power meter.

## Limits

These are estimates. GPS accuracy, missing elevation, gaps, wind, e-bike
assistance, and your personal efficiency all affect them. A partial estimate
shows which part of the track it covers. A missing estimate is not zero.

See [energy models](../../architecture/analysis/energy.md) for how they are
calculated.
