# Plan routes and places

Plan a new route, or save places you want to visit. **More info** on the
Planner start screen shows examples.

## Plan a route

1. Open **Planner → Start a new plan**.
2. Choose **Draw route**, then a **Travel mode**: touring bike, road bike,
   mountain bike, hiking, or car.
3. Click the map to add points. The route follows paths between them.
4. Click **Save**.

Distance, climb, time, and an elevation profile update as you edit. Your
recorded tracks and public trails are shown for reference only; the route
follows your points and the travel mode.

## Change the route

- **Move a point:** drag it, or click it and choose **Move to…**, then click the
  new position.
- **More options:** click a point to **Remove point**, **Start route here**, or
  **End route here**.
- **Tools:** **Reverse route** or **Clear route**. Clearing the route keeps your
  saved places.
- **Undo** and **Redo** work for all changes.

Keyboard: with the map focused, **Alt+Left/Right** selects route points and
**Enter** opens their options.

Drag the panel down to see more of the map. **More map** shrinks it to the
totals; **Show tools** brings the tools back.

## Save places

Choose **Points of interest** to add named places with an icon and an optional
note. A plan can contain a route, places, or both.

## Open a saved plan

On the Planner start screen:

- **Continue your current plan** returns to your unsaved draft.
- **Continue a saved plan** opens a saved plan to edit it or export it as GPX.

**Save copy** saves your changes as a new plan and keeps the original.

GPX export contains only the route, not the places. Plans don't count in your
activity statistics.

If routes don't finish, see [route planning service](../administration/services/routing.md).
