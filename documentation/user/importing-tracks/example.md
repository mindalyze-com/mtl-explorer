# Try the example archive

Use the [example archive](../../../docs/examples/synthetic-archive/synthetic-tracks.zip)
to test MTL Explorer without your own tracks. It contains twelve made-up loops:
eight from June 2024 and four from June 2025. They are drawn near the 0,0
coordinate and are not real routes.

1. Download and unzip the archive.
2. Upload its GPX files in **Settings → Import & sync**.
3. Wait for **Settings → Processing** to finish, then click **Reload** when asked.
4. In **Filter**, select each year and compare its tracks and statistics.

All positions, times, and elevations are made up. The examples are provided
under the repository's AGPL-3.0 license.
