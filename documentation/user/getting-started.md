# Get started

You need a running MTL Explorer. [Install it](../administration/installation/quick-start.md),
or try the [public demo](https://mtl-demo.mindalyze.com/mtl/).

The public demo already contains tracks. Uploads, manual rescans, Garmin sync,
issue acknowledgements, source review decisions, and re-assessment are unavailable
there. Track, media, and planner edits remain available. Start at step 4.

1. Open your installation's `/mtl/` address and sign in.
2. Open **Settings → Import & sync** and upload a few GPS files. You can also
   copy them into the server's GPS folder.
3. Open **Settings → Processing** and wait until the import is done. If the map
   asks you to reload, click **Reload**.
4. Open **Filter** and choose which tracks to show, for example a year or an
   activity type.
5. Click a track on the map. If several tracks overlap, choose one from the list.
6. Look at the track's tabs:
   - **Overview** and **Graphs**: distance, time, elevation, and speed.
   - **Quality**: why a value is missing or unreliable.
   - **Media**: photos and videos from that activity.
   - **Events**: breaks and GPS gaps.
7. Open **Statistics** to see your totals and trends.

Want to try it without your own data? Use the
[example archive](importing-tracks/example.md).

Don't see a track? It may still be processing, be hidden by a filter, or need
review. See [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
