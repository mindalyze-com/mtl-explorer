# Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| A track I imported is missing | Check **Settings → Processing**. Check your filter. Open **Review** for files that failed or were hidden. |
| Statistics don't match the map | Check your filter. Some tracks may be hidden from statistics in **Review**, or lack time or elevation data. |
| A photo or video is missing | Run **Settings → Maintenance → Rescan Media**. Check the photo's date and whether **All media** or **Track related** is selected. Zoom out on the map. |
| A photo is in the wrong place | Check the camera time and [correct it](media/capture-time.md), or [set the location](media/locations.md). |
| Heart rate is missing | Google Health history may still be loading. Heart rate needs a track with timestamps. |
| Replay is greyed out | Replay needs a track with timestamps and no big gaps. |
| Route planning doesn't finish | Routing data may still be downloading. Check **Settings → Processing**. |
| The map shows old data | Click the data indicator and choose **Reload**. |

## Ask for help

Note what you did, the error you saw, and the app version. **Settings → System
information → Copy all versions** copies the version details. Don't share
personal files or passwords.

For server problems, see [administrator troubleshooting](../administration/troubleshooting.md).
