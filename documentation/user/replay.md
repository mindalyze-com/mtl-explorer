# Replay tracks

## Replay one activity in 3D

1. Open a track's details and choose **3D Replay**.
2. Use Play/Pause and the progress slider.
3. Open **Settings** to change the duration, camera, smoothing, shown data, or
   other tracks in view. **Hide settings** closes them again.
4. **Stop** goes back to the start; **Close** returns to the normal map.

Tracks without usable timestamps replay by distance.

## Replay several attempts

To race attempts of the same stretch against each other, select them in
[Segments](analysis/segments.md) and choose **Replay**. Each attempt keeps its
color and label. In a [comparison](analysis/comparison.md), **Open Replay here**
starts at the point you are looking at.

## Animate your archive

**Animate** shows your tracks appearing on the map over time, for the tracks in
your current filter.

1. Set the **Date range** and **Playback speed**.
2. Press Play.

**Reset** goes back to the beginning; **Stop** opens the settings again. On a
phone, expand the playback panel to reach the settings.

Replay does not fix gaps or change your statistics.
