# Browse tracks

Open **Statistics → Tracks** to see all tracks in your current filter as a list.
Search, sort, and page through them. Small route sketches help you recognize
tracks.

- Click a track name or description to open its [details](details.md).
- Use a row's map button to show the track on the map.
- Click **More** to read a long description, **Less** to shorten it.

Track missing? Change or reset your filter, check **Settings → Processing**, and
open **Review**. Several recordings of the same activity may be marked as
duplicates. See [Review](source-review.md).
