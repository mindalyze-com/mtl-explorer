Dark theme persistence

- Selected Dark in Admin Preferences.
- Before reload: data-theme=dark.
- Normal browser reload: data-theme=dark; body background rgb(10, 10, 15).
- Credentials-only sign-out: /mtl/login retained data-theme=dark and rgb(10, 10, 15).
- Valid sign-in: /mtl/ retained data-theme=dark and rgb(10, 10, 15).
- No theme preference had to be selected again.
