Viewport: 1280 x 720

Panel maximize
- Started with Details hidden and Nearby collapsed.
- Maximize panel filled the full MTL Explorer viewport and changed its action
  to Restore panel size.
- Details remained hidden and Nearby remained collapsed.
- In maximized mode, Details opened, Nearby expanded, Next moved 1/6 to 2/6,
  and wheel zoom reached scale(2.05443) with Reset zoom.
- A second maximized cycle hid Details and collapsed Nearby. Restore panel size
  returned to the normal viewer with both states still hidden/collapsed.

Browser fullscreen blocker
- The adjacent Enter fullscreen action was activated once through the semantic
  browser control and once by direct trusted pointer input.
- Both attempts cleared panel maximize as required but did not enter browser
  fullscreen: document.fullscreenElement stayed null, no Exit fullscreen action
  appeared, and document.fullscreenEnabled was unavailable in this in-app
  browser context.
- The still-open viewer retained Details/Nearby and photo 2/6 after each
  attempt. Because the browser Fullscreen API is unavailable, changing states
  inside true fullscreen and exiting by action/Escape cannot be exercised.
