GLB_01 automatic globe entry

- Started from the Zürich map at 100 m scale with the globe control outside its visible zone.
- Used the normal Zoom out control 15 times, allowing each transition to settle.
- At 500 km the globe control was visible but reported `aria-pressed=false`.
- Continued zooming out; at 1000 km the same control reported `aria-pressed=true` and gained the `mtl-globe-active` class.
- Zoom, rotation, terrain, location search, filter, and navigation controls remained available.
