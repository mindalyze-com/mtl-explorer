Comparison

Review timing and effort together.

Compare time, speed, and heart rate between the same checkpoints. The attempt marked R is the reference. The first chart shows how far ahead or behind each attempt was at the same distance.

Move along a chart to see the matching spot on the map. Ghosts show where the other attempts were when the reference reached that spot.

Open Replay here starts a replay of the attempts at that spot.

Check what the timing can support.

Comparing times needs tracks with timestamps. Recordings also differ in how often they saved a position.

Elapsed time and stops

Times include stops between the checkpoints. The finish difference compares each attempt with the reference. The same distance can be at a different place on the ground, for example after a detour.

Gaps and missing times

Gaps or missing timestamps can leave an attempt without a time. A missing time is not zero.

Zone placement matters

Large checkpoints can match a different part of a track than you expect. Check the result on the map and add checkpoints in between if needed.

Graphs depend on available data

Speed needs positions and times; elevation needs recorded heights. Power and energy are estimates. Heart rate needs data from the optional Google Health connection.
