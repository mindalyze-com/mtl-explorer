Optional Google Health connection

Bring your health records into your own archive.

MTL Explorer can request read-only Google Health access and copy selected records into the MTL Explorer installation you operate.

The connection stays installation-specific.

MTL Explorer does not operate a central Google Health account or data service.

Separate OAuth client

Each administrator creates a Google Cloud project and Desktop OAuth client for their own installation.

Read-only access

The connector requests read-only scopes. It does not add, edit, or delete records in Google Health.

Direct connection

The self-hosted server exchanges tokens and reads records directly from Google. The MTL Explorer project does not receive them.

Local controls

The administrator can disconnect Google, remove the local mirror, and revoke access from the Google Account.

Data requested by MTL Explorer.

The connector requests four Google Health read-only categories. The user reviews these permissions during Google consent.

Activity and fitness

Exercise, activity, steps, distance, energy, and related summaries supported by the API.

Health measurements

Available body measurements, observations, vital signs, and exact heart-rate samples.

Sleep

Sleep sessions and their available stages or measurements.

Location

Route points attached to exercise records. Valid routes can be imported as locally managed tracks.
